What each partner mark actually means.

A logo on a course site implies endorsement. Sometimes it is one, sometimes it is a licence to use the tool, and usually nobody tells you which. Here is the exact scope of each mark we display.

Our rule: every mark on this site has a stated scope, and nothing beyond that scope is claimed. No logo appears here to imply accreditation, certification of our programme, or an employment relationship.

The marks we display

PartnerStated scopeWhat it does not mean
Lovable Official Solutions Partner. We build and teach with Lovable, and hold partner status under their programme. It does not mean Lovable endorses our assessments or guarantees outcomes for our candidates.
OpenAI We use OpenAI models in our curriculum, in WorkSim simulations and in internal tooling. It does not mean OpenAI accredits, endorses or partners with our certification.
Anthropic (Claude) We use Claude models in our curriculum and in WorkSim simulations. It does not mean Anthropic accredits, endorses or partners with our certification.

If a relationship changes

This table is updated on the day a scope changes, not when the next brochure is printed. If a partnership ends, the mark comes off the site the same week — including from pages that were generated earlier.

Why this page exists

Because "official partner" is a phrase with no defined meaning across the industry. Two companies can both say it and mean completely different things: a signed commercial agreement, a licence to use a logo, or a reseller arrangement. The only way for you to know which one you are looking at is for the provider to say so in writing.

If you need documentation for any mark here, write to hello@thecodingcompany.io and ask. We would rather answer the question than have you guess.
