Why software & it professionals need role-based AI certification

Generic AI courses teach everyone the same prompts. Role-based certification starts from your job: the workflows you own, the decisions you make, and the outcomes your team measures. Employers in software & it are looking for people who can point at a shipped result, not a completion certificate.

How the four tiers work

Foundation proves AI literacy for any role. Practitioner proves you apply AI in daily workflows. Strategist proves you can lead AI initiatives and evaluate trade-offs. Executive proves you can drive AI transformation across an organisation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI certification for software & it professionals?

There is no single answer, because the best credential depends on your exact job title. TCC CERT+ publishes 84 role-based AI certifications across 21 software & it roles, each in four tiers: Foundation (no AI background needed), Practitioner (applying AI in daily workflows), Strategist (leading AI adoption) and Executive (owning AI strategy and governance). Start at the CERT+ catalogue, type your job title, and TCC resolves it to the nearest of its 262 roles.

Are there AI courses for software & it professionals?

Yes. TCC CERT+ offers 84 certifications for 21 software & it roles, from Foundation to Executive, plus in-depth AI courses. Each credential is built around the workflows, decisions and outcomes of that specific role rather than generic AI theory.

Do software & it professionals need to know how to code to use AI?

No. The Foundation tier assumes no prior AI or coding background and covers practical AI tools for the role. Later tiers go deeper into applied workflows, evaluation and strategy as needed.

Which roles in software & it does TCC cover?

Each one has a Foundation, Practitioner, Strategist and Executive credential.

Does TCC help software & it professionals get hired?
