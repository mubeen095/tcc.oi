Why insurance & risk professionals need role-based AI certification

Generic AI courses teach everyone the same prompts. Role-based certification starts from your job: the workflows you own, the decisions you make, and the outcomes your team measures. Employers in insurance & risk are looking for people who can point at a shipped result, not a completion certificate.

How the four tiers work

Foundation proves AI literacy for any role. Practitioner proves you apply AI in daily workflows. Strategist proves you can lead AI initiatives and evaluate trade-offs. Executive proves you can drive AI transformation across an organisation.
