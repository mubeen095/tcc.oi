What we don't claim.

A list of things we could say, and will not. Every item below is a claim a competitor makes in good faith or bad — and in both cases it makes the buyer worse off, because it removes the ability to tell providers apart.

01 We do not guarantee a job.We provide preparation, assessment and employer introductions. Hiring decisions belong to employers, and any provider promising you an offer is either lying or charging you for a lottery ticket.
02 We are not a government-accredited institution.TCC CERT+ is an industry credential issued by The Coding Company. It is not a UGC-recognised degree, a government licence or a regulated professional qualification.
03 We do not publish a placement percentage yet.No cohort has completed the placement window. See the publication rule instead of a number.
04 We do not promise a salary.We can show you what roles pay in the market and what the work requires. We cannot promise what a company will offer you.
05 We do not claim a deeper partnership than we have.Each partner mark on this site has a stated scope. Read the exact wording rather than inferring an endorsement.
06 We do not use stock photos as alumni.Every face on this site will be a real person who agreed to be there, or there will be no face.
07 We do not sell your data.Your details go into our lead system and nowhere else. No list rental, no data brokers, no "partners who may contact you".
08 We do not run fake scarcity.If a cohort has seats left, we say how many are actually left. If it is full, it is full — we do not reopen it because the ad worked.
09 We do not use countdown timers on price.The fee is the fee. A price that expires in nine minutes is a pressure tactic, not an offer.
10 We do not hide the refund terms.Seven days, in writing, on the refund page, in plain language. If it were complicated, it would not be a refund.

Why publish this at all

Because the alternative is that you have to work it out yourself, usually after paying. A provider's non-claims tell you more about how it will behave when something goes wrong than its promises do — promises are written by marketing, and the failure plan is written by whoever has to live with the consequences.

If you find us making any claim on this list anywhere on this site, in an ad, or in a sales call, that is a bug. Send it to hello@thecodingcompany.io and it gets fixed and acknowledged.

What we do claim

A defined assessment, a published rubric, a format disclosed before you pay, worked examples at three levels of quality, and a paper you can sit in fifteen minutes to decide for yourself. That is a smaller list than most, and every item on it is checkable.
