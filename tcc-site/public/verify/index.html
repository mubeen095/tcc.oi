Everything we claim, and how to check it.

Most course websites are built so that nothing can be checked. This page is the opposite. Every claim we make is listed here next to the evidence for it — and if a claim is not on this page, treat it as marketing rather than fact.

What this page can and cannot do

It cannot prove that we are good at teaching. No website can, and any site claiming otherwise is selling certainty it does not have.

It can prove that we are checkable: that our scoring is defined before you sit it, that our format is disclosed, that our numbers have a stated method, and that the things we do not do are written down rather than left to implication.

For most people deciding where to spend a year, checkability is worth more than confidence. Confidence is cheap to fake. Checkability is not.

Start with the paper

The fastest way to audit us is to sit the diagnostic. Fifteen questions, fifteen minutes, scored the moment you submit, with the reasoning shown for everything you get wrong. You will know more about how we think in fifteen minutes than in an hour of reading.
