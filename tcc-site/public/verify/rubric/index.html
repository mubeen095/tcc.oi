How we mark things.

Two things get a mark at TCC: the diagnostic you can sit right now, and the CERT+ assessments. Both are defined here in full. Nothing is marked on impression.

1 · The diagnostic

Fifteen questions across four sections, one mark each, no negative marking, fifteen-minute limit. The split is fixed:

SectionQuestionsMarksWhat it measures
Quantitative44Percentage change, rates, averages, sequential probability — the arithmetic that actually appears in placement papers
Reasoning55Sequences, set overlap, syllogism, inference from a passage, analogies
Technical44Complexity, language semantics, SQL null behaviour, HTTP status codes
Data interpretation22Reading a rate correctly, locating the real drop-off in a funnel

The bands are set by what the score means for the next eight weeks of work, not by a curve. We do not rescale results to make them look better.

ScoreBandWhat it means
13–15Tier-1 readyYou would clear most aptitude rounds today. Remaining work is speed and interview delivery.
10–12Close, with named gapsTwo or three specific topics are costing you marks. Closeable in weeks.
6–9Foundation needs structured workYou know more than you can execute under time pressure. The most common and most improvable profile.
0–5Start from the mechanicsLearn the underlying rules before practising papers, or you lock the errors in.

2 · CERT+ assessments

Practitioner and above are marked out of 100 across four criteria. The weights are fixed and published before the cohort starts.

CriterionWeightWhat earns marks
Applied knowledge30Correct, specific use of the concepts the role actually requires. Not definitions — decisions.
Role artifact45A working output a colleague could use on Monday. Correct, complete, and honest about its limits.
Judgement and trade-offs25Why this approach over the alternative, what you gave up, and what would change your mind.

Pass rule: 60 of 100 overall, and no criterion below 40% of its weight. A brilliant artifact attached to thoughtless judgement fails, because that is the profile that damages teams in production.

3 · Performance bands

The same four descriptors are used on every criterion. Reviewers pick the band first, then place the score inside it.

BandRangeDescriptor
Distinction85–100Would pass review at a good company without supervision. Explains its own limitations and the conditions under which it fails.
Competent70–84Works, is defensible, and has one clear gap the candidate can name.
Developing55–69Right direction, incomplete execution, or correct output with weak reasoning.
Not yet0–54Misunderstands the problem, or produces output that would need to be redone.

4 · How we stop reviewers drifting

  • Every artifact is scored blind. The reviewer sees a candidate ID, never a name.
  • Ten percent of artifacts are double-marked by a second reviewer.
  • If the two marks differ by more than ten points, a third reviewer resolves it and the rubric wording is reviewed.
  • Whenever a rubric line is rewritten, previously marked samples are re-marked against the new wording so the standard does not quietly move.

This is ordinary assessment hygiene. It is published because most providers do not publish it, and the absence of a method is usually the point.
