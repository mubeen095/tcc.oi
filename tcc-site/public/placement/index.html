What it is

What is TCC Placement Partner?

Our AI-focused placement program is built for Indian college students. We focus on practical readiness, portfolio proof and company-pattern practice — so you don't just learn AI, you demonstrate it.

Practise first — Prep+

The practice arm of Placement

Prep+ is open to everyone. Six tracks cover the rounds that actually decide offers — sit a timed paper, find the marks you are losing, then drill the topics behind them.

Track 01

Aptitude

Quant, reasoning, data interpretation and verbal ability — with worked solutions, not just answer keys. Open aptitude → Track 02

Programming

Python, Java, C++, JavaScript, SQL and C — language drills built for a live coding round. Open programming → Track 03

DSA

Arrays, strings, hashing, trees, graphs and dynamic programming — sequenced by pattern. Open DSA → Track 04

Technical MCQs

DBMS, OS, networking, OOP, plus AI and machine-learning fundamentals — the theory rounds most candidates skip. Open MCQs → Track 05

Company patterns

Round structure, syllabus, eligibility and the topics that carry the most weight across 71 Prep+ company patterns. Open company papers → Track 06

Mock tests

Sit a full timed paper, get a score and a per-topic breakdown, then revise what cost you marks. Open mock tests →

Questions

AI placement program — FAQ

What is TCC Placement Partner?

TCC Placement Partner is a placement program for Indian college students. We help you build role-relevant proof, get interview-ready and prepare for AI internships and entry-level roles.

Is TCC Placement Partner a job guarantee?

No. We do not guarantee jobs. We provide career support, mock interviews and company-pattern practice. See our outcome publication rule.

What if I haven't done CERT+ yet?

You can start with Prep+ practice to build interview readiness, or take a project through Gigs to build proof of work. CERT+ is still the credential path when you are ready to target a role.

Who is eligible for placements?

Indian college students who have completed a TCC CERT+ certification relevant to their target role or a TCC in-depth course, and who meet the portfolio-readiness checklist.

Do I need a degree to get an AI internship or job through TCC?

If a role asks for a degree, show the projects that back it up. If it does not, your portfolio and interview performance do the work.

How long does placement support last?

Our four-step timeline runs from learning and proof-building to interview prep and applications. Support continues while you are actively applying.

Does TCC help with AI internships for students?

Yes. Internships are a core entry path. Read our guide on how to get an AI internship in 2026.
