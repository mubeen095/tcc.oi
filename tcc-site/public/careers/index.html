How we hire

Application

A CV and one piece of evidence relevant to the role. A campaign, a dashboard, a process note, a call recording — something you can talk about. No cover letter template.

Work sample

A short, scoped exercise that mirrors the job. We tell you the time box in advance and we do not ask for unpaid production work.

Conversation

Two interviews: one on craft, one on judgement and working style. You will meet the person you would report to, not a panel of strangers.

Decision

We aim to reply to every complete application within 10 working days. If we are slower, we will tell you why and give you a date.

What we look for

  • Evidence. A claim about your ability should come with something a stranger can inspect.
  • Clarity. You can explain your work in plain English, including the parts that went wrong.
  • Ownership. You notice a broken handover and fix it, rather than routing it to a meeting.
  • Judgement with AI. You use AI tools, verify their output, and disclose what you used.

Pay, location and practicalities

Roles are remote-first in India, with an office in Hyderabad for people who want one. We share the compensation band before the first interview, and the final offer depends on the level and the evidence you bring. Internships carry a stipend and a defined six-month scope.

We do not ask for unpaid production work, and we do not keep a role open to collect CVs. If a role closes, the page is updated before the next build.

How to apply: email careers@thecodingcompany.io with the role title in the subject, your CV, and one link or attachment that shows the work. Tell us the level you are applying for and the level you think you are not ready for yet. If email is not practical, use the contact page and put the role in the message.
